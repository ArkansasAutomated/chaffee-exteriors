## Handling replies (decision tree)

- **"Send the one-sheet"** → send PDF same day, add to quarterly check-in list.
- **"We have a vendor"** → reply: "Totally fair — if they're ever booked out or a listing is in Van Buren/Alma/Greenwood where they're slow to reach, keep my number." Then move to a 90-day check-in.
- **"What do you charge agents?"** → agents pay nothing; the seller/homeowner pays published prices. Never offer kickbacks or referral fees — say so plainly if asked.
- **Property managers specifically** → pivot to per-property gutter programs and turnover clean-outs, quoted per property in writing.
- **Home inspectors specifically** → pivot to item 2 only: fast written quotes and quick turnaround on flagged gutter/downspout items so closings don't slip.

**Compliance notes:** No competitor names, no prohibited claims, no invented results ("we've helped dozens of closings" is banned until it's true — use [REVIEW PLACEHOLDER]). Every message ends with the site or phone CTA. No fake urgency — the cadence itself is the persistence.
