# 08 — First 10 Customers: 14-Day Plan (DRAFT for Dre)

Goal: **10 paid jobs in 14 days, spending $0–$300.** Math: 10 jobs × avg ticket ≈ $250–$600 = $2,500–$6,000 revenue from a few hundred dollars of hustle. The plan leans on three free channels (doors, groups, network) plus one optional paid boost.

**Ground rules (kit rules + honesty rules):**
- No fake reviews, no buying reviews, no discounts-for-reviews, no incentives tied to reviews — review asks come only after a completed job, via 10-job-ops.md.
- No fake urgency: "free 15-minute check" is the offer; nothing expires.
- One post per group, intro tone, group rules first. If a group forbids promo posts, don't post — comment helpfully instead and move on.
- Door hangers go on doors of homes only — never in mailboxes (federal law), never inside gates.

**Budget ($0–$300):**
| Item | Cost |
|---|---|
| 300 door hangers (print, from 06 spec) | ~$120–$150 |
| Optional: one $40–$60 boost on the best-performing intro post | $0–$60 |
| Fuel for route days | ~$40 |
| Buffer | ~$50 |
| **Total** | **≤ $300** |
